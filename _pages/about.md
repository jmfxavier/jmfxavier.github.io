>“Most of the time I'm just sitting there, in an almost detached manner, thinking, 'Well, here's another day's wastebasket full of paper. Nothing's come through. Maybe another day. Maybe I should stay up tonight and try some more.' I stay up nights when my wife and children have gone to sleep. Over and over again I keep working at it, trying to understand something which after months or even years turns out to be so simple that I should have seen it in the first ten minutes. Why does it take so long?"

H. Robbins, *Mathematical People: Profiles and Interviews*
